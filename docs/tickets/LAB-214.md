# LAB-214: A routine sample sometimes never gets a result

**Type:** bug · **Component:** sample-service · **Reporter:** lab operations (night shift)

## What happened

On 21 October the night shift submitted routine sample `S-310044` (assay K). The service
accepted it (`"status":"queued"`), but the sample never got a result: no `result` event, no
error, no cancel. The tube was found on the analyzer in the morning, unmeasured.

The same happened three times in the last two weeks, always on busy nights. Nobody has
been able to reproduce it on purpose.

## What else we noticed

- When the STAT sample took a place on the rack, the log line says
  `preempted S-319007 at position 4`: the ID of the STAT sample itself, not of the sample
  that had to leave the rack.
- In the same cycle there is a `result` line with an empty `sample_id`, no assay and the
  flag `E`. Nobody knows which tube it belongs to.
- The STAT sample `S-319007`, submitted that night, came out one cycle later than expected.

The complete output of the service from that night (anonymized, same build as production)
is attached as `LAB-214-service-output.jsonl`.

## Expected

Every accepted sample is measured exactly once, or is reported as cancelled.
